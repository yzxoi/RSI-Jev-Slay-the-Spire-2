# E072 — stronger immediate HP-loss penalty in combat planning

Issue: [#136](https://github.com/yzxoi/RSI-Jev-Slay-the-Spire-2/issues/136). Baseline: merged `origin/main` `0ab503b2a225d2aac1c031d4d13bff4a4b9d5253`. The E069/E071 baseline headless cohorts had 0/28 wins; 13/14 A10 runs ended in Act 1, with seven A10 normal-monster defeats entering their last fight at median 21% maximum HP. These are exploratory traces, not a win-rate estimate. The current planner scores immediate damage at 0.85 per point and forecast HP loss at 1.5 per point.

Hypothesis: increasing only the forecast HP-loss coefficient from 1.5 to 3.0 will preserve health and yield deeper complete runs, particularly on A10. `retaliate` is the unchanged baseline; opt-in `retaliate_hp3` uses the same Jev macro prompt, legal candidates, search width/depth, trigger and retaliation rules. No game value, RNG, reward or victory edits.

Frozen evaluation before implementation: pinned game v0.111.0, sts2-cli `084d1aa3d8e118ca7ce8d8774ad16d6be9c92367`, Jev `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917`; seeds `e072_loss_001` through `_004`, Ironclad and Silent, A0 and A10, both policies: 16 pairs / 32 complete-run episodes. Each episode has at most 1500 steps and 180 seconds; four workers share 5000 Jev calls / $1.50. Command: `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_loss_e072`. Save all raw traces under ignored `artifacts/runs/`, publish compact per-run outcomes, exact code/dependency hashes, and verified trace SHA-256 values.

Decision: implementation tests must pass, all 32 episodes must finish without a new execution error, and treatment must produce at least 20 same-state combat choices different from the baseline planner. Promote only if at least four pairs reach a deeper act/floor, at most two regress, at least two A10 pairs improve with at most one A10 regression, and no observed baseline victory becomes a loss. Otherwise close as negative or inconclusive. Even a positive 16-pair result needs replication and native validation. Shadow choices on treatment states measure policy exposure, not counterfactual battle outcomes.

Scope boundary: twin-crab `SURROUNDED_POWER` and `CRAB_RAGE_POWER` mechanics require a separate frozen combat experiment. This experiment changes only generic HP-loss weighting in the headless policy.

Implementation iteration at exact tested SHA `ea937a4d71c67677382db4b08970c4814ed63b49` added opt-in `retaliate_hp3` without changing baseline `retaliate` or native controller behavior. The only score difference is forecast HP-loss coefficient 3.0 versus 1.5. Treatment computes a shadow baseline action on its same observed state and traces each different first action. `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -q` passed 113 tests, including an attack/block tradeoff and a lethal-kill control. The pinned local CLI has original game DLL SHA-256 `9cb4f1ad8c9f284aa8fec3122ffd6d780bbf543d875c817abdd12ff63fbf12b4`; complete patch/assembly hashes and the exact model/dependency metadata are in [`result.json`](result.json).

The frozen command `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_loss_e072` ran all 32 configs. All 32 ignored raw trace SHA-256 values verified. Outcomes: 31 normal defeats, zero wins, one simulator error; 823 Jev calls cost $0.132114276 provider-reported, with zero usage-uncertain calls. The baseline Ironclad A0 `e072_loss_003` reached Act 2 floor 11 before an `event_choice` failed with `MissingMethodException: Method not found: 'Godot.Vector2I Godot.Vector2I.get_One()'`; its treatment died normally at Act 1 floor 17. This pair is **incomplete**, not a battle loss or a valid depth comparison. All selected runs, including the error, exact code SHA, act/floor, costs and trace hashes are in [`result.json`](result.json).

The 15 complete pairs yielded three treatment improvements, five regressions and seven ties. Among eight A10 pairs, two improved, three regressed and three tied. Treatment had 221 same-state first-action differences, well above the exposure minimum; 172 were Attack-to-Skill first-action changes, including 50 Strike-to-Defend and 13 Bash-to-Defend. These are action observations, not proof that delaying attack caused a later defeat. The simplest global HP weight may overvalue immediate block relative to removing future attackers or boss damage checks. Small paired runs and Jev's stochastic macro choices limit causal attribution.

Decision: **close the E072 PR without merging `retaliate_hp3`**. The predeclared compatibility rule failed because of one separate CLI event error, and the strategy promotion rule failed independently: three improvements versus required four, five regressions versus allowed two, and three A10 regressions versus allowed one. Keep the negative policy and raw traces on this branch. A follow-up should fix simulator compatibility in its own issue if that event must be studied; tactical improvement should estimate multi-turn threats or specific encounter mechanics instead of increasing one global loss coefficient.
