# E089 — independent held-out replication of E088 threat budget

Issue: [#170](https://github.com/yzxoi/RSI-Jev-Slay-the-Spire-2/issues/170). Baseline: merged `retaliate` at `b22c28ebed9342fa81a0c94109118024fb226d04`.

E088's treatment improved terminal act/floor in 8/20 exploratory A10 pairs and worsened two, but did not add an Act 2 entry, so its PR was closed. This experiment copies the **unchanged** tested E088 controller policy and resource-check module (E088 gameplay SHA `1f362222f02dbebd2ef340d22879f8f953d4dc37`) into a fresh branch from main. Only the evaluator cohort/label/budget differ. It authorizes at most one potion at the first material Act 1 threat, with Jev selecting from legal potion/target candidates and exact inventory verification. No HP, reward, RNG or victory edit. E088's 20 pairs are not included in the held-out decision count.

Fresh, fixed seeds `e089_holdout_001`–`_006`, Ironclad/Silent/Defect/Regent/Necrobinder, Ascension 10: 30 matched pairs / 60 complete-run CLI episodes. Four workers; each episode max 2,000 decisions/300 seconds. Shared Jev budget 6,000 calls/$1.50 with exact-request `MatchedJev`. Game v0.111.0, sts2-cli `084d1aa3d8e118ca7ce8d8774ad16d6be9c92367`, Jev `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917`. Command: `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_budget_holdout_e089`. Raw traces and stdout remain ignored; compact results and verified hashes go in `result.json`.

Decision rule: all 60 raw trace hashes verify, 30/30 initial states match, every first differing action is a same-state legal potion use, every use removes exactly one potion, no more than one authorization per combat, and all 60 runs end normally. Exposure: at least 30 treatment uses across at least 20 pairs. Strength: treatment deeper in at least ten pairs, shallower in at most four, no baseline win-to-loss, and at least one additional Act 2 entry. If all gates pass, merge only as an opt-in policy pending broader/native validation; otherwise close. These cohorts cannot estimate a robust win rate.

## Frozen held-out result

The unchanged E088 policy, tests and fixed evaluator were committed as `4c64f8bf12dab89df102b4b3354188389724ec54` **before** game execution. `rsi/full.py` SHA-256 `fc29a8acd372cd209e9e9dd2fd568790bd0632a159ef0eee2c51d9b06b1d7dcf` and `rsi/resource_check.py` SHA-256 `ef9be31ac29aa982730386b375d13f191e162e12ba2b5598feeca48f63193c53` match E088's tested source files byte-for-byte. `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -q` passed 116 tests. Exact game command: `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_budget_holdout_e089 > artifacts/runs/e089-eval-stdout.log 2>&1`. Original game DLL SHA-256 `9cb4f1ad8c9f284aa8fec3122ffd6d780bbf543d875c817abdd12ff63fbf12b4`; the pinned headless assembly, patches, dependencies, all selected configs/results, and trace hashes are in [result.json](result.json).

All **60/60** episodes ended in normal defeat, without error, stall or victory. All 60 raw trace hashes verified; all 30 pairs had the same initial-state hash. Every pair first diverged on a same-state legal potion action, zero elsewhere. The treatment used **59** potions across all 30 pairs, each followed by exactly one inventory decrement; at most one was authorized per combat. Thus execution fidelity and exposure passed.

Terminal act/floor: **8 deeper, 0 shallower, 22 tied**. By character: Ironclad 0/0/6; Silent 2/0/4; Defect 3/0/3; Regent 1/0/5; Necrobinder 2/0/4. Act 1 Boss entries were 13 baseline versus 14 treatment; Act 2 entries were **2 versus 3**. Neither arm won. Shared `MatchedJev` budget: 822 attempted calls, $0.128816772 provider-reported/budgeted spend, zero uncertain calls. The one extra Act 2 entry is Silent `_006` (Act 1 floor 17 baseline to Act 2 floor 4 treatment). E088's exploratory 8/2/10 pairs are not counted in this held-out 8/0/22 result.

Trace-level Boss diagnosis: of the 13 baseline and 14 treatment Act 1 Boss entrants, **11 per arm died in that Boss battle**. In each arm, 10/11 Boss defeats entered with at least 50% max HP. All 11 baseline and 10/11 treatment Boss defeats entered holding at least one potion. At the last recorded combat decision, several Bosses still had over 100 HP; one treatment Ironclad `_004` Boss had only five HP left after 13 rounds. These are descriptive trace observations, not a claim that every defeat has the same cause. They indicate that better early potion timing alone is insufficient; Boss output/scaling and some late-battle tactics remain concrete bottlenecks.

No real game was operated; E081 remains paused/unconfirmed. The threat budget is programmatic, not an Astra model call per floor. Current-hand planner loss is approximate, not a full battle forecast. Same terminal floor can hide HP/resource differences, and paired branches can diverge through subsequent choices and RNG.

Decision: **close without merging under the frozen rule**. All fidelity/exposure gates, zero-worse condition and the additional Act 2 entry passed, but the held-out direction gate required at least ten deeper pairs and observed eight. This is a favorable exploratory signal, not a preregistered promotion. Mainline `retaliate` stays unchanged. A future experiment should separately evaluate Boss preparation or exact-state tactical counterfactuals; further threshold changes based on these same seeds would overfit.
