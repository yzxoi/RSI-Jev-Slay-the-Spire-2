# E075 — target-aware Weak mitigation in approximate turn planning

Issue: [#142](https://github.com/yzxoi/RSI-Jev-Slay-the-Spire-2/issues/142). Baseline: merged `origin/main` `0ab503b2a225d2aac1c031d4d13bff4a4b9d5253`. The current planner adds a fixed Weak utility but keeps the target's current displayed attack unchanged. E072/E073 showed that increasing global HP-loss weight did not improve overall depth; this experiment tests a concrete mechanics correction instead.

Hypothesis: forecast the target's **current-turn** Weak attack reduction per hit, only when the status will apply, and use that in the turn's HP-loss calculation. The baseline is unchanged `retaliate`; opt-in treatment `retaliate_weak` uses the same Jev macro prompt, candidates, width/depth and all other combat rules. No game value, RNG, reward or victory edits. Do not claim that a correct immediate forecast also predicts future turns.

Frozen mechanic audit: [`frozen-weak-transitions.json`](frozen-weak-transitions.json) contains 241 eligible observed targeted Weak transitions from all 64 E072/E073 ignored traces, whose SHA-256 values were verified against their published results. Eligibility requires a live targeted enemy before/after, current attack intent, no pre-existing Weak or Artifact, and observed post-action Weak. Compare static pre-action intent damage against the treatment's predicted per-hit damage and actual immediate post-action intent. Require at least 50% lower aggregate absolute error and at least 70% exact matches; report every mismatch. This is observation-based mechanic validation, not a causal battle outcome.

Frozen strength evaluation: pinned game v0.111.0, sts2-cli `084d1aa3d8e118ca7ce8d8774ad16d6be9c92367`, Jev `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917`; fresh seeds `e075_weak_001` through `_004`, Ironclad and Silent, A0 and A10, both policies: 16 pairs / 32 complete-run CLI episodes. At most 1500 steps/180 seconds per episode; four workers share 5000 Jev calls/$1.50. Command: `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_weak_e075`. Preserve all raw traces under ignored `artifacts/runs/` and publish exact code/dependency hashes, outcomes and verified trace hashes.

Decision: unit controls must cover multi-hit, existing Weak, Artifact and nonattacking target; all 32 episodes complete without a new execution error and treatment has at least 20 same-state first-action changes. Promote only if mechanic audit passes, at least four pairs reach a deeper act/floor with at most two regressions, at least two A10 pairs improve with at most one A10 regression, and no observed baseline victory becomes a loss. Otherwise close as negative/inconclusive. The sample is exploratory, not a stable win-rate estimate. Crystal Sphere visual event failures are simulator errors, not battle outcomes.
