# E066 — funded-shop relic purchase guard

Issue: [#124](https://github.com/yzxoi/RSI-Jev-Slay-the-Spire-2/issues/124). Baseline: merged `main` at `c456815c6b0f51c6034f510362fe7a087f29fa9e`. E065's real Ironclad A0 trace shows Jev left the floor-20 shop with 305 gold and three affordable relics, then died on floor 33 with 444 gold. This is a missed spending opportunity, not proof that a particular purchase would have saved the run.

Hypothesis: while a shop inventory is open, if gold is at least 250, no purchase has been made in that shop, and one or more stocked relics are affordable, restricting Jev's next choice to those legal relic purchases improves full-run progress. After the first accepted purchase, restore all ordinary options. This opt-in rule has no card/deck or HP edits and never invents an item. It may be wrong when saving for a later shop is valuable, so the baseline is the existing Jev macro policy, not a straw-man fixed shop policy.

Frozen paired evaluation: pinned headless game v0.111.0, sts2-cli `084d1aa3d8e118ca7ce8d8774ad16d6be9c92367`, Jev `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917`; policies `retaliate` and `retaliate_shop_relic`, seeds `e066_shop_001`, `e066_shop_002`, `e066_shop_003`, characters Ironclad and Silent, ascensions A0 and A10. This is 12 pairs/24 episodes, at most 1500 actions and 180 seconds per episode, four workers, shared budget 3000 Jev calls/$1.50. No seed is selected by observing outcomes. Record all statuses, act/floor/HP, victory, accepted relic purchases, trigger exposures, gold, calls/cost, exact tested SHA, dependency hashes and raw trace SHA-256. Replay the frozen E065 floor-20 shop state offline to check that the rule proposes only legal affordable relics; that replay is not a battle outcome.

Decision rule: promote opt-in only if all episodes complete without new execution errors, at least six pairs encounter the guard, at least two exposed pairs improve in act/floor depth, at most one exposed pair regresses, and no observed baseline victory becomes a loss. Otherwise close as inconclusive/negative while retaining iteration history and trace hashes. Small N does not establish win rate; native compatibility and native strength remain separate claims.

Implementation iteration 1 at tested SHA `2b97b6d26b7b49b565070753d5406ce1a7079b69`: shared opt-in candidate filter in headless/native controllers, with per-shop accepted-purchase tracking; no default behavior change. `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -q` passed 108 tests. [`frozen-replay.json`](frozen-replay.json) applies the committed rule to the actual E065 floor-20 shop state (`source_state_hash=602b5213...`, source raw trace SHA-256 `7bd151c4...`): exactly three advertised relic purchases priced 169–249 remain at 305 gold; leaving is restored after one purchase. This verifies candidate legality only, not a counterfactual win.
