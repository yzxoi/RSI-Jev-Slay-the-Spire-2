# E077 — A10 critical-HP combat weight replication

Issue: [#146](https://github.com/yzxoi/RSI-Jev-Slay-the-Spire-2/issues/146). Baseline: merged `origin/main` `a7c2469f86777f2cef2d1bef6fd68ac30fe42ca7` with E076's card-selection request cap. E073's exploratory A10 subgroup had 2 deeper, 0 shallower and 6 tied matched pairs; its complete mixed-A0/A10 cohort failed the overall rule and had two A0 simulator errors. The subgroup is a lead to replicate, not evidence of general improvement.

Hypothesis: at A10, increasing the forecast HP-loss coefficient from 1.5 to 3.0 **only when current HP/maxHP ≤0.40** improves run progression. The baseline is the merged `retaliate` policy. Opt-in `retaliate_lowhp` retains the same macro Jev prompt, legal choices, planner width/depth, triggers and retaliation; the low-HP coefficient alone differs. No HP, rewards, RNG or victory values are edited.

Frozen evaluation before implementation: seeds `e077_a10_001` through `_008`, Ironclad and Silent, A10, baseline and treatment: 16 matched pairs / 32 full-run CLI episodes. Each episode is limited to 1,500 steps and 180 seconds. Four workers share 5,000 Jev calls / $1.50. Pinned game v0.111.0, sts2-cli `084d1aa3d8e118ca7ce8d8774ad16d6be9c92367`, Jev `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917`. Command: `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_lowhp_e077`. Commit implementation before evaluation; preserve every ignored raw trace and publish compact per-run outcomes, exact code/dependency hashes, costs and verified trace hashes.

Decision: all 32 episodes must finish as a normal victory or defeat with zero execution errors; at least 10 same-state first-action differences at or below 40% HP and zero above; ≥5 matched pairs deeper, ≤2 shallower and no baseline victory turned into a loss. Merge the opt-in policy only if all criteria pass. Otherwise close as negative/inconclusive. Progression at this sample size is exploratory, not a win-rate estimate; any positive result requires broader and native validation.

## Implementation and full-run evaluation

The opt-in policy, same-state action audit, tests and exact frozen evaluator were committed as `cc3dfd2ae5e39118fb1502e06a7dcd0dfc21a955` **before** evaluation. `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -q` passed 115 tests, including healthy-HP, critical-HP and lethal-kill controls. `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_lowhp_e077` ran all 32 configs with the preregistered limits. The original local game DLL SHA-256 was `9cb4f1ad8c9f284aa8fec3122ffd6d780bbf543d875c817abdd12ff63fbf12b4`; exact headless assembly, patches, SDK and model dependency metadata are in [result.json](result.json).

All 32 episodes ended in a **normal defeat** in Act 1; zero victories, execution errors or stalls. All 32 ignored raw trace files were SHA-256 verified, with each relative path and digest in [result.json](result.json). The treatment changed 51 first actions on the same state at or below 40% HP and 0 above the threshold. Among 16 matched pairs, 2 treatment runs reached a deeper floor, 2 a shallower floor and 12 tied. Ironclad had 1 better, 1 worse, 6 tied; Silent likewise had 1 better, 1 worse, 6 tied. The improvements were Ironclad `_002` floor 14→17 and Silent `_008` floor 6→7; regressions were Silent `_001` floor 15→14 and Ironclad `_005` floor 13→7. The shared Jev budget recorded 664 calls, $0.099311184 provider-reported cost, no unknown-usage calls. No baseline victory existed to convert into a loss.

Decision: **close the E077 PR without merging**. Execution and action-scope criteria passed, but the preregistered progression requirement of at least 5 deeper pairs failed (2 observed). This independent A10 replication does not support promoting the low-HP coefficient. Pairing fixes seed/character/ascension, but the two complete-run trajectories can diverge after a changed action or independently sampled Jev macro response; the differences are run outcomes, not a proof that every changed action caused them. The repeated first-Act losses point to a larger early-run bottleneck than this coefficient alone addresses.
