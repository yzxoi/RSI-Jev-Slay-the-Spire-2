# E080 — held-out Act 1 Boss buff policy

Issue: [#152](https://github.com/yzxoi/RSI-Jev-Slay-the-Spire-2/issues/152). Baseline: merged `origin/main` `8ea843d434a64fedd3247a2dafd6f6092fab8293`, policy `retaliate`. E079 replayed two selected E077 Boss entries and one Ironclad branch cleared the Boss after Liquid Bronze + Dexterity. That is selected battle evidence, not an estimate of new-seed run strength.

Hypothesis: an opt-in `retaliate_boss_buffs` policy that spends legal battle-long buff potions on **Act 1 Boss round 1**, before any card, improves held-out A10 complete-run progress. Priority is Liquid Bronze, Dexterity Potion, Strength Potion, Fysh Oil; use each available potion in that order with a fresh state read between actions. All other potions remain reserved. The treatment otherwise uses the same Jev macro prompt, legal rewards/routes, combat planner, search parameters and RNG as baseline. No HP, rewards or victory values are edited.

Frozen held-out evaluation before implementation: seeds `e080_boss_001`–`_012`, Ironclad and Silent, A10, baseline and treatment: 24 matched pairs / 48 complete-run CLI episodes. Four workers, up to 4,000 decisions and 600 seconds per episode; shared 10,000 Jev calls / $2.00. Use MatchedJev to cache identical macro requests. Pinned game v0.111.0, sts2-cli `084d1aa3d8e118ca7ce8d8774ad16d6be9c92367`, Jev `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917`. Command: `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_boss_buffs_e080`. Preserve ignored raw traces and verify every SHA-256 value; publish exact tested SHA/dependencies, first-intervention state/action-prefix parity, potion consumption, all episode status/act/floor, model usage and failures.

Decision: all 48 episodes must end normally without execution error; at least two treatment pairs must execute a legal Boss buff from exactly the same pre-intervention state and action prefix as baseline, with confirmed inventory decrease; no-intervention pairs must remain identical. At least two pairs must progress farther and at most one regress, including at least one treatment entering Act 2 when baseline did not. No baseline victory may become a loss. Only then merge as an opt-in candidate for broader/native testing. Otherwise close as negative or inconclusive. This sample is not a win-rate estimate or an Act 3 victory claim.

## Held-out complete-run result

Opt-in implementation, tests and frozen evaluator were committed as `c7b5a060c4b449ae1d85ebf305cb44fef9fe498b` **before** evaluation. `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -q` passed 116 tests. `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_boss_buffs_e080` executed all 48 preregistered CLI episodes; complete stdout is ignored under `artifacts/runs/e080-eval-stdout.log`. Original local game DLL SHA-256: `9cb4f1ad8c9f284aa8fec3122ffd6d780bbf543d875c817abdd12ff63fbf12b4`. Exact assembly/patch/dependency hashes, every configuration, outcome, action/entry parity audit and verified raw trace path/hash are in [result.json](result.json).

All 48 episodes ended in **normal Act 1 defeat**: zero victories, execution errors or stalls. All 24 initial states matched by pair; all 48 raw decision trace hashes verified. MatchedJev produced 500 paid calls and 500 same-request cache hits, with $0.075402516 provider-reported cost and zero unknown-usage calls. All 24 pairs tied on terminal act/floor. In the 23 pairs with no policy intervention, the full selected-action/state-hash sequence and terminal status matched exactly between arms. This makes the result interpretable as low exposure, not evidence that a triggered potion has no effect.

Four treatment runs entered the Act 1 Boss. Three carried no eligible battle-long buff: Ironclad `_003` had Swift Potion/Snecko Oil, `_005` Flex Potion/Ashwater, `_009` Clarity Extract. The only active pair, Ironclad `_008`, entered Vantom at 77 HP with Strength Potion/Stable Serum. Its first 82 actions and pre-intervention state hash `adda211f4f738322897a8f73497c9f1c1ba46626aa7e09ab2b4aef6bfbea7b4d` matched baseline exactly; the treatment used Strength Potion and the next state confirmed one fewer potion. Both arms died at Act 1 floor 17, Boss round 7. At the last observed combat decision Vantom had 116 HP with baseline and 62 HP with treatment, while player HP was 17 and 2 respectively. Greater damage did not produce a Boss clear or deeper run in this case.

Decision: **close without merging**. Compatibility and matching checks passed, but the preregistered exposure gate failed (1 active pair versus ≥2) and the progression gate failed (0 deeper pairs versus ≥2; 0 new Act 2 entries). This 24-pair cohort shows the narrow rule rarely finds an eligible potion in practice. It does not refute E079's selected same-state Boss clear, nor justify deploying the rule in native play. A broader floor-level resource plan would need to handle the actual potion inventory and early-Act survival, then face a new preregistered paired test.
