# E069 — retest funded-shop guard after simulator relic fix

Issue: [#130](https://github.com/yzxoi/RSI-Jev-Slay-the-Spire-2/issues/130). Baseline: merged `main` at `9810da2b30c7c589c5c1ce53f50e9c8fbd390720`. E066's two exposed headless treatment runs failed inside the simulator's relic-purchase log on floor 14, before there was a valid strategy outcome. E067 repaired that local simulator path. E068 then proved native shop review and a state-bound purchase work, but its one visible run lost on floor 33 and does not estimate a shop-policy gain.

Hypothesis: with E067's pinned simulator repair, the opt-in E066 funded-shop relic guard can make those two advertised purchases and continue to a normal whole-run outcome. This retest ports the exact E066 headless candidate restriction and its per-shop purchase tracking. It does not change the merged default `retaliate` policy, HP, rewards, RNG or victory.

Frozen inputs: Jev `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917`, game v0.111.0 and sts2-cli `084d1aa3d8e118ca7ce8d8774ad16d6be9c92367`; seeds `e066_shop_001`, `_002`, `_003`, Ironclad and Silent, A0 and A10, both `retaliate` and `retaliate_shop_relic`. This is 12 pairs/24 full-run episodes, max 1500 actions and 180 seconds per episode, four workers, shared 3000 Jev calls/$1.50. [`frozen-shop-floor20.json`](frozen-shop-floor20.json) is the original E065 native shop compatibility snapshot from E066, copied unchanged. Record every status, act/floor/HP/gold, accepted relic purchases, guard exposures, exact code/dependency SHA, model cost and verified raw trace hash. The batch command is `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_shop_e069`.

Decision: repair compatibility passes only if the Ironclad A0 and Silent A0 `e066_shop_002` treatments each accept a legal relic purchase and finish without the previous simulator error. Promote the shop guard for strength only if all 24 episodes complete without a new execution error, at least six pairs are exposed, at least two exposed pairs improve depth with at most one regression, and no observed baseline victory becomes a loss. Otherwise retain the compact evidence and close the PR as inconclusive for policy strength, while keeping reusable simulator compatibility code already merged. Differences among unexposed pairs are not attributed to the guard. A 12-pair batch is exploratory, not a win-rate estimate.

Implementation iteration at exact tested SHA `41b1c216d9dcb17dfe8992953ffcb26e5fd9b7ef` ported the E066 opt-in headless rule without changing its 250-gold threshold, one-purchase-per-shop behavior or candidate filtering. `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -q` passed 110 tests, including the frozen native shop candidate replay. The test confirms candidate legality, not battle strength. The repaired headless assembly SHA-256 was `ad5fc0f5958958d5a0473b81525642bbc8ddff33ea07d90fe675b6f3fdfc99ad`; the original game DLL SHA-256 was `9cb4f1ad8c9f284aa8fec3122ffd6d780bbf543d875c817abdd12ff63fbf12b4`. The precise patch and dependency hashes are in [`result.json`](result.json).

The fixed batch command `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_shop_e069` ran all 24 frozen configs and verified 24/24 ignored raw trace SHA-256 values. All 24 episodes ended as normal defeats: zero wins, execution errors or stalls. Across 12 pairs, the act/floor comparison was 10 ties, one treatment deeper and one treatment shallower. Only **two treatment episodes** encountered the funded-shop guard, both on seed `e066_shop_002` at A0. Ironclad accepted a 175-gold Whetstone and reached act 2 floor 4, while its baseline reached act 1 floor 17. Silent accepted a 192-gold Blood Vial and reached act 1 floor 17, while its baseline reached act 2 floor 8. Those opposite exposed-pair outcomes do not support promoting the guard. The other ten pairs had no guard exposure and provide no causal guard signal.

The previous E066 `Buy relic failed: Object reference not set to an instance of an object` error did not recur; both formerly crashing configurations bought their advertised relics and finished. This meets the repair-compatibility hypothesis. The shared model budget recorded 663 calls, $0.109688838 provider-reported spend, $0.009408 estimated for unavailable usage, and seven uncertain model calls; the total is therefore not a fully provider-reported charge. The batch stayed below its 3000-call/$1.50 cap. [`result.json`](result.json) lists every selected seed, character, ascension, policy, outcome, cost and trace hash, including failures if any.

Decision: close this experiment PR without merging the forced-purchase headless policy. The predeclared strength rule required at least six exposed pairs and at least two exposed improvements; observed exposure was two with one improvement and one regression. E067's simulator fix is already merged and this branch preserves the successful retest traces. A stronger shop policy should compare concrete items and downstream risk on a larger exposed cohort instead of assuming any affordable relic is worth buying.
