# E073 — critical-HP-only combat loss penalty

Issue: [#138](https://github.com/yzxoi/RSI-Jev-Slay-the-Spire-2/issues/138). Baseline: merged `origin/main` `0ab503b2a225d2aac1c031d4d13bff4a4b9d5253`. E072's global increase from 1.5 to 3.0 changed 221 same-state actions yet yielded three deeper/five shallower/seven tied valid pairs. Most changes delayed attacks to play Skills, including many at healthy HP. Its separate simulator error is not a battle outcome.

Hypothesis: apply the 3.0 forecast HP-loss coefficient only at current HP/maxHP ≤0.40 and retain the default 1.5 above that threshold. This may preserve critical A10 lives without slowing early offense. `retaliate` stays unchanged; opt-in `retaliate_lowhp` uses the same Jev macro prompt, legal candidates, search width/depth, triggers and retaliation. No HP, RNG, reward or victory edits.

Frozen evaluation before implementation: pinned game v0.111.0, sts2-cli `084d1aa3d8e118ca7ce8d8774ad16d6be9c92367`, Jev `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917`; new seeds `e073_lowhp_001` through `_004`, Ironclad and Silent, A0 and A10, both policies: 16 pairs / 32 complete-run episodes. Each has at most 1500 steps and 180 seconds; four workers share 5000 Jev calls / $1.50. Command: `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_lowhp_e073`. Preserve every ignored raw trace and publish exact code/dependency hashes, compact per-run outcomes and verified SHA-256 values.

Decision: implementation tests pass; all 32 episodes complete without an execution error. Require at least 20 same-state first-action differences at or below 40% HP and zero action differences above that threshold. Promote only if at least four pairs reach a deeper act/floor, at most two regress, at least two A10 pairs improve with at most one A10 regression, and no observed baseline victory becomes a loss. Otherwise close as negative or inconclusive. This exploratory cohort cannot estimate win rate; any positive result requires replication and native validation. An event `Godot.Vector2I.One` failure, if repeated, is a separate simulator compatibility result.

Implementation at exact tested SHA `1c4c1cb4146b2e69b10fbde048e53717cc7d3c1c` added only opt-in `retaliate_lowhp`: the planner's loss coefficient is 3.0 at current HP/maxHP ≤0.40, otherwise unchanged at 1.5. Treatment shadows the baseline first action on the same state. `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -q` passed 113 tests, including healthy, critical and lethal-kill controls. The exact local original game DLL SHA-256 was `9cb4f1ad8c9f284aa8fec3122ffd6d780bbf543d875c817abdd12ff63fbf12b4`; all other pinned assembly, patch and model/dependency metadata are in [`result.json`](result.json).

The frozen command `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_lowhp_e073` ran all 32 configs and verified 32/32 ignored raw trace SHA-256 values. Outcomes: 30 normal defeats, zero victories and **two simulator errors**. Both errors were treatment Ironclad A0 runs at `e073_lowhp_001` (Act 3 floor 3) and `_002` (Act 2 floor 3), each at an `event_choice` with the repeated `MissingMethodException: Method not found: 'Godot.Vector2I Godot.Vector2I.get_One()'`. Neither is a battle defeat or a valid paired comparison. Jev made 826 calls for $0.137108748 provider-reported, with zero usage-uncertain calls. All selected inputs, per-run act/floor/status/cost and raw trace hashes are in [`result.json`](result.json).

The 14 complete pairs yielded three deeper, three shallower and eight tied treatment outcomes. Eight A10 pairs yielded two deeper, zero shallower and six ties; this small subgroup is exploratory and cannot override the frozen overall rule. There were 57 same-state first-action changes at low HP and **zero above the threshold**, so scope worked. Of those changes, 43 changed an Attack opener to a Skill opener, including 24 Strike-to-Defend. An action change is not proof of downstream causation; Jev macro decisions and game trajectories may diverge after it.

Decision: **close the E073 PR without merging**. The all-episode compatibility criterion failed from two repeated CLI errors, and the strength rule failed independently: three improvements versus required four, and three regressions versus allowed two. The A10 signal merits future larger replication only after CLI compatibility is repaired. Preserve all raw traces and this negative result on the closed branch. The repeated missing Godot API should be a separate simulator issue before another broad full-run strength experiment.
