# E073 — critical-HP-only combat loss penalty

Issue: [#138](https://github.com/yzxoi/RSI-Jev-Slay-the-Spire-2/issues/138). Baseline: merged `origin/main` `0ab503b2a225d2aac1c031d4d13bff4a4b9d5253`. E072's global increase from 1.5 to 3.0 changed 221 same-state actions yet yielded three deeper/five shallower/seven tied valid pairs. Most changes delayed attacks to play Skills, including many at healthy HP. Its separate simulator error is not a battle outcome.

Hypothesis: apply the 3.0 forecast HP-loss coefficient only at current HP/maxHP ≤0.40 and retain the default 1.5 above that threshold. This may preserve critical A10 lives without slowing early offense. `retaliate` stays unchanged; opt-in `retaliate_lowhp` uses the same Jev macro prompt, legal candidates, search width/depth, triggers and retaliation. No HP, RNG, reward or victory edits.

Frozen evaluation before implementation: pinned game v0.111.0, sts2-cli `084d1aa3d8e118ca7ce8d8774ad16d6be9c92367`, Jev `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917`; new seeds `e073_lowhp_001` through `_004`, Ironclad and Silent, A0 and A10, both policies: 16 pairs / 32 complete-run episodes. Each has at most 1500 steps and 180 seconds; four workers share 5000 Jev calls / $1.50. Command: `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_lowhp_e073`. Preserve every ignored raw trace and publish exact code/dependency hashes, compact per-run outcomes and verified SHA-256 values.

Decision: implementation tests pass; all 32 episodes complete without an execution error. Require at least 20 same-state first-action differences at or below 40% HP and zero action differences above that threshold. Promote only if at least four pairs reach a deeper act/floor, at most two regress, at least two A10 pairs improve with at most one A10 regression, and no observed baseline victory becomes a loss. Otherwise close as negative or inconclusive. This exploratory cohort cannot estimate win rate; any positive result requires replication and native validation. An event `Godot.Vector2I.One` failure, if repeated, is a separate simulator compatibility result.
