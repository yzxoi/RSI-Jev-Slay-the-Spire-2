# E078 — Act 1 threat-horizon potion use

Issue: [#148](https://github.com/yzxoi/RSI-Jev-Slay-the-Spire-2/issues/148). Baseline: merged `origin/main` `a7c2469f86777f2cef2d1bef6fd68ac30fe42ca7`, policy `retaliate`. The E077 A10 baseline ended 15/16 runs with potions still held, 34 in total, including all four boss defeats. E016's earlier broad per-action Jev potion access had 2 better/4 worse/9 tied A10 pairs, so merely adding potion candidates to every model call is not supported.

Hypothesis: a programmatic reserve rule improves A10 Act 1 survival by spending known battle-long buff potions at the first-Act boss opener and known rescue potions when the unchanged planner predicts current-turn HP loss at least as large as current HP. Treatment `retaliate_potion_horizon` keeps macro Jev, normal card planner, rewards and routes identical; it inserts a legal, engine-exported potion action only at those triggers, then observes the resulting state and replans. Unknown and one-turn setup potion effects remain reserved. The first-Act scope makes the inter-floor cost explicit; no HP, rewards, RNG or victory flags are edited.

Frozen evaluation before implementation: seeds `e078_potion_001`–`_008`, Ironclad and Silent, A10, baseline/treatment: 16 matched pairs / 32 full-run CLI episodes. Limit each to 1,500 steps/180 seconds. Four workers share 5,000 Jev calls / $1.50. Pinned game v0.111.0, sts2-cli `084d1aa3d8e118ca7ce8d8774ad16d6be9c92367`, Jev `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917`. Command: `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_potion_e078`. Preserve raw ignored traces, verify every hash and commit compact per-run results, exact tested SHA/dependency hashes, potion timing/use and costs.

Decision: all 32 episodes must finish as normal victories or defeats without execution errors; treatment must use at least four legal potions; at least four pairs must reach deeper progress and at most two shallower, with no observed baseline victory becoming a loss. Merge only if all pass, as an opt-in policy pending broader and native validation. Otherwise close without promotion. Separate compatibility, potion utilization and full-run strength claims.
