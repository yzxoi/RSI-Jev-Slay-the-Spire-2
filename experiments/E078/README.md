# E078 — Act 1 threat-horizon potion use

Issue: [#148](https://github.com/yzxoi/RSI-Jev-Slay-the-Spire-2/issues/148). Baseline: merged `origin/main` `a7c2469f86777f2cef2d1bef6fd68ac30fe42ca7`, policy `retaliate`. The E077 A10 baseline ended 15/16 runs with potions still held, 34 in total, including all four boss defeats. E016's earlier broad per-action Jev potion access had 2 better/4 worse/9 tied A10 pairs, so merely adding potion candidates to every model call is not supported.

Hypothesis: a programmatic reserve rule improves A10 Act 1 survival by spending known battle-long buff potions at the first-Act boss opener and known rescue potions when the unchanged planner predicts current-turn HP loss at least as large as current HP. Treatment `retaliate_potion_horizon` keeps macro Jev, normal card planner, rewards and routes identical; it inserts a legal, engine-exported potion action only at those triggers, then observes the resulting state and replans. Unknown and one-turn setup potion effects remain reserved. The first-Act scope makes the inter-floor cost explicit; no HP, rewards, RNG or victory flags are edited.

Frozen evaluation before implementation: seeds `e078_potion_001`–`_008`, Ironclad and Silent, A10, baseline/treatment: 16 matched pairs / 32 full-run CLI episodes. Limit each to 1,500 steps/180 seconds. Four workers share 5,000 Jev calls / $1.50. Pinned game v0.111.0, sts2-cli `084d1aa3d8e118ca7ce8d8774ad16d6be9c92367`, Jev `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917`. Command: `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_potion_e078`. Preserve raw ignored traces, verify every hash and commit compact per-run results, exact tested SHA/dependency hashes, potion timing/use and costs.

Decision: all 32 episodes must finish as normal victories or defeats without execution errors; treatment must use at least four legal potions; at least four pairs must reach deeper progress and at most two shallower, with no observed baseline victory becoming a loss. Merge only if all pass, as an opt-in policy pending broader and native validation. Otherwise close without promotion. Separate compatibility, potion utilization and full-run strength claims.

## Implementation and fixed CLI evaluation

The Act 1 reserve/rescue policy, tests and evaluator were committed as `9397aeec5cab84b5acf795e534a011388a369043` before evaluation. `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -q` passed 117 tests. `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_potion_e078` ran all 32 frozen configs with the preregistered limits. Original local game DLL SHA-256 was `9cb4f1ad8c9f284aa8fec3122ffd6d780bbf543d875c817abdd12ff63fbf12b4`; pinned assembly/patch/SDK/model metadata and exact per-run configs are in [result.json](result.json).

Thirty-one episodes ended in normal Act 1 defeat, zero in victory, and **one baseline episode** (`Silent`, `_006`) stopped at Act 1 floor 5 when a Jev `map_select` call raised `RemoteDisconnected: Remote end closed connection without response`. This was a model transport failure in the unchanged baseline, not a potion action. It remains an error, not a defeat or valid pair. All 32 ignored raw trace SHA-256 values were rechecked and are listed with relative paths in [result.json](result.json). The shared Jev budget made 595 calls, with $0.095529756 provider-reported cost and $0.009408 conservatively estimated across seven calls with unknown usage; neither amount should be presented as an exact combined charge.

The treatment issued nine potion actions, **all nine followed by a verified decrease in potion inventory**. Four were Weak Potion, three Block Potion and two Shackling Potion, all from projected-lethal rescue. No boss-long-buff action was exercised: only one treatment episode reached the Act 1 boss, carrying Fire and Colorless Potions rather than the known persistent buffs targeted here. Thus this cohort tests the rescue branch, not the boss-opener branch. Of 15 valid pairs, treatment was deeper in three, shallower in two and tied in ten. The deeper pairs were Ironclad `_003` floor 7→13, Silent `_004` 7→11 and Silent `_005` 6→8; the shallower pairs were Silent `_002` 9→6 and Silent `_003` 7→5. Every normal run still died in Act 1. No baseline victory existed to convert into a loss.

Decision: **close without merging**. Legal use and inventory-transition evidence pass, but the full-run strength gate fails independently (3 deeper versus required ≥4), as does the all-normal execution gate because of the baseline model transport error. The boss-opener hypothesis is not evaluated by this sample. Direct Jev potion exposure (E016) and this bounded rescue policy are distinct negative/inconclusive results; neither supports a native strength claim. A targeted boss-state experiment and a separate model transport retry fix would be more informative than silently rerunning this cohort.
