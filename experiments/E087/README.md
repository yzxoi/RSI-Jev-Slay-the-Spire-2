# E087 — one early resource decision per risky A10 fight

Issue: [#166](https://github.com/yzxoi/RSI-Jev-Slay-the-Spire-2/issues/166). Baseline: merged `retaliate` at `b22c28ebed9342fa81a0c94109118024fb226d04`.

In E084's 20 A10 baseline traces, **all 20** entered their fatal combat holding potions: 15 with two, four with one, one with three. The baseline planner never uses them. E016's broad Jev access had 2 deeper/4 shallower/9 tied valid pairs; E078's projected-lethal rescue yielded 3 deeper/2 shallower/10 tied and one baseline model error. Neither passed its strength rule. E086's extra card-reward prompt also failed. This experiment tests a different, narrower decision: one explicit potion-vs-reserve choice **early in a risky encounter**, when a potion may prevent attrition rather than only rescue predicted death.

Treatment `retaliate_early_resource` keeps the merged macro Jev and card planner. In Act 1, a legal potion allows one model request at round 1 of an Elite or Boss, or at the first attacking state of an ordinary Monster when HP is at most 45% of maximum and the unchanged planner predicts at least five HP loss that turn. The model sees current state, known Boss, HP, room, forecast and legal potion/target candidates plus reserve. If it uses a potion, the controller verifies inventory fell by exactly one and replans from the fresh state. At most one resource decision occurs per combat. The threshold and wording are experimental heuristics; they do not claim accurate future battle simulation. No HP, rewards, RNG or victory flags are edited.

Frozen cohort: fresh seeds `e087_resource_001`–`_004`, all five characters, Ascension 10, baseline and treatment = 20 matched pairs / 40 complete-run CLI episodes. Four workers; each episode max 2,000 decisions/300 seconds. Shared Jev budget 4,000 calls/$1.00 with exact-request matching. Game v0.111.0, sts2-cli `084d1aa3d8e118ca7ce8d8774ad16d6be9c92367`, Jev `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917`. Command: `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_early_resource_e087`. Raw traces stay ignored; compact per-run results and trace hashes go in `result.json`.

Decision rule: all 40 raw trace hashes verify, 20/20 initial states match, every first differing action is a legal same-state potion use, actual inventory decrements for every use, at most one resource decision per combat, and all 40 runs end normally. Need at least four treatment potion uses, treatment deeper in at least five pairs, shallower in at most two, no baseline win becoming defeat, and at least one additional Act 2 entry. A pass justifies merging only an opt-in policy for broader/native validation. Otherwise close without promotion. This is exploratory, not a win-rate estimate.

Implementation and evaluation SHA, exact command, versions, all outcomes, limits and decision will be added after testing.
