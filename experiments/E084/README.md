# E084 — deck-job guidance at A10 card rewards

Issue: [#160](https://github.com/yzxoi/RSI-Jev-Slay-the-Spire-2/issues/160). Research input: [E083](../../docs/research/deck-jobs-v0111.md). Baseline: merged `origin/main` SHA `59ab5f2b7c564bf4ea7f1fb9c350f4b88ea35119`, policy `retaliate`.

Hypothesis: a compact current-state deck summary plus threat-first rubric at card rewards will improve A10 complete-run progress over the generic Jev `STRATEGY` prompt. Treatment `retaliate_deck_jobs` changes only the Jev context at `card_reward`; route, shop, event, rest, combat policy and legal candidates stay the same. The summary contains descriptive counts, not fixed card rankings or adequacy thresholds. Neither policy edits HP, rewards, RNG or victory flags.

Frozen cohort: seeds `e084_jobs_001`–`e084_jobs_004`, all five characters (Ironclad, Silent, Defect, Regent, Necrobinder), Ascension 10, baseline and treatment = 20 matched pairs / 40 CLI complete-run episodes. Four workers. Each episode stops at normal victory/defeat, 2,000 decisions or 300 seconds. Shared Jev budget: 4,000 calls / $1.00; identical requests use `MatchedJev`. Pinned game v0.111.0, sts2-cli `084d1aa3d8e118ca7ce8d8774ad16d6be9c92367`, Jev `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917`. Command after committing the implementation: `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_deck_jobs_e084`.

Audit and promotion rule: verify all 40 trace hashes and every pair's initial-state parity. The first different selected action, if any, must be a card reward choice from the same state; any other first divergence is a matching/execution failure. All 40 episodes must end normally; at least six pairs must first diverge at a reward; treatment must reach a deeper act/floor in at least five pairs, be shallower in at most two, cause no baseline victory to become a loss, and produce at least one additional Act 2 entry. Only then merge the opt-in policy for broader held-out/native validation. Otherwise close as negative or inconclusive. This small cohort is exploratory, not a win-rate estimate.

## Implementation and result

Implementation and frozen evaluator were committed as `4cfb1b661bb900fa7e9861637d0fc404c89171a1` **before** evaluation. `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -q` passed 114 tests. Exact evaluation command: `python3 -m scripts.evaluate_deck_jobs_e084 > artifacts/runs/e084-eval-stdout.log 2>&1`. The original local game DLL SHA-256 was `9cb4f1ad8c9f284aa8fec3122ffd6d780bbf543d875c817abdd12ff63fbf12b4`; headless game assembly, patches, SDK and model dependency metadata are in [result.json](result.json). The raw stdout and all raw traces remain ignored; the result file lists every selected character/seed/policy and verified trace hash.

All **40/40** episodes ended in normal Act 1/2 defeat: zero victories, execution errors or stalls. All **20/20** pairs had identical initial-state hashes, and all **40/40** raw decision trace hashes matched. Thirteen pairs first diverged on a **same-state card reward**; zero first diverged elsewhere. Seven pairs followed identical state/action sequences. This confirms the treatment was applied at the intended decision scope, not that its choices were better.

Terminal act/floor: treatment deeper in **3** pairs, shallower in **2**, tied in **15**. By character: Ironclad 1 better/1 worse/2 tied; Silent 0/0/4; Defect 1/0/3; Regent 1/1/2; Necrobinder 0/0/4. Baseline entered an Act 1 Boss in five runs and Act 2 in one; treatment entered an Act 1 Boss in four runs and Act 2 in zero. The baseline's Regent `_004` reached Act 2 floor 7, while treatment died at Act 1 floor 17 after a floor-12 first reward divergence (Manifest Authority → Solar Strike). The Ironclad `_001` first diverged on floor 3 (Eternal Armor → Mind Blast), then ended at floor 17 versus 11. These examples locate possible regressions; later paths also diverged, so they do not prove each card caused the outcome. Positive first divergences included Defect `_001` (Charge Battery → Leap, floor 8→11) and Ironclad `_003` (Bludgeon → Headbutt, floor 9→11).

The shared MatchedJev budget recorded 461 attempted calls, **$0.068516112 provider-reported cost**, **$0.069860112 budgeted spend** including a conservative $0.001344 unknown-cost reserve, 173 cache hits and one transient `URLError` retried successfully. The arms' wall times and costs differ because their trajectories and cache hits differ; this is not speed evidence. No real game was operated. The source E081 game remains paused/unconfirmed.

Accounting iteration: the first evaluator output mislabeled the reserve-inclusive budget as provider cost. Reporting-only fix `35391cc` was committed after the 40 gameplay episodes; `python3 experiments/E084/reconcile_budget.py` relabeled the stored result and verified that the gap equals the reserved estimate. No policy, trace, outcome or sample changed, and the cohort was not rerun. Tested gameplay SHA remains `4cfb1b6`.

Decision: **close the E084 PR without merging**. Fidelity/execution and exposure gates passed, but the preregistered progression threshold failed (3 deeper versus ≥5), and there was no additional Act 2 entry (0 versus ≥1). A generic jobs prompt with coarse deck counts is not supported as a stronger A10 policy by this cohort. A narrower next test should replay exact pre-reward states from the divergent pairs and compare threat-specific plans through the next battle; it requires its own issue and cannot use offline choice disagreement as a success measure.
